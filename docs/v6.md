# Atmovio 6.0.5 upgrade notes

The new workspace groups everyday work into Cameras, AI Detections, My Videos and Studio. Clips are ordered by recording start time, newest first, even when older footage is exported or merged later. Advanced AI evidence, filters and processing details are collapsed. The interface remains Czech.

- Camera playback uses HLS for live view and recorded video. Mark OD–DO on the device-local timeline to save up to six hours. Gaps remain visible; saving available footage across an outage requires an explicit checkbox. Playback maps elapsed media time back to the recording clock.
- Overlapping automatic clips from one camera are joined into a bounded range. Old clips stay available until the replacement is ready; missing source recordings prevent consolidation. Sources referenced by saved Studio projects are retained by automatic cleanup.
- Studio saves editable projects, including trim, speed, music offset/volume/fades, a versioned intro, text and subscribe/share callouts. Upload reusable MP4/MOV/WebM animations (up to 30 s / 100 MB), remove green or black backgrounds and preview them over a source thumbnail. Effects use automatic or manual timing, optional original sound and adjustable size. Position video effects with X/Y percentages or drag the bounding box in the preview; keyboard arrows move by 1%, Shift + arrow by 10%. The project retains the coordinates for playback and rendering. Music library removal supports single/bulk trash, restore and purging tracks not used by saved projects. Active render jobs keep their tracks. Music volume shows 0–200% and applies to audition playback; music fades apply during rendering. Re-editing a rendered video creates another version. Publishing is a separate action.
- The updater validates a copy of the configuration with Frigate 0.18.2 before stopping the old recorder. It backs up the stopped database and configuration, then checks the new API and previously active cameras. Failure restores the old image, configuration and database. Media is not moved. The restart causes a short recording gap.
- Backups live under `/opt/nvr/atmovio/backups/`. Diagnostics group repeated errors, support resolved status and export redacted JSON. Review free text before sharing a diagnostic export.

See the [Czech walkthrough](cs/verze-6.0.md) for the full user flow, and [installation](install.md) for requirements.

Update older installations through **Nastavení → Systém → Aktualizace → Zkontrolovat teď → Nainstalovat verzi 6.0.5**. The updater downloads the release asset and verifies its checksum. Studio switches to a full-width music or intro workspace when that tool is selected; the source video stays in the trimming tool.
